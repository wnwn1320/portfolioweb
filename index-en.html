Angel Core

A 15-second AI visual film completed within a limited four-hour production window, from image generation and animation through final editing.
I used ChatGPT to create the initial storyboard images and prompts, Google Flow to generate the video, and Premiere Pro for pacing, cut editing, background music, and post-production.
By combining liminal-space imagery with the Angelcore aesthetic, I created scenes in which an otherworldly angel appears within realistic environments.

This project began with the question: [Can generative AI be used to build a consistent character and world within a short production time?]

When generating multiple scenes with AI, a character's appearance often changes and the overall visual tone can become inconsistent. I wanted to test whether these limitations could be addressed while still producing one cohesive film.

Production was limited to four hours and the final video to 15 seconds. The goal was to create a short visual film that maintained the same protagonist and world throughout, while evaluating the potential and efficiency of a workflow spanning image generation, animation, and editing.

  • Visual concept and character development
  • AI image generation and prompt design
  • Scene-to-scene consistency refinement for the character, costume, and backgrounds
  • Video generation, final editing, and color grading
